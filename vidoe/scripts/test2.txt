### The Cat Who Stole the Fish

One day, there was a little cat named Milo.

Milo loved fish very much.

One morning, he saw a beautiful aquarium in his neighbor’s house. Inside the aquarium were many colorful fish.

Milo looked at the fish and said,
“Wow! They look delicious!”

When nobody was watching, Milo quietly jumped onto the table.

He put his paw into the aquarium and tried to catch a fish.

But the fish were too fast!

Milo tried again and again.

Finally, he caught one small fish and ran away.

But suddenly, the neighbor saw him.

“Hey! You little thief!” she shouted.

Milo ran as fast as he could.

He jumped over the fence and hid behind a tree.

The fish was still in his mouth.

Milo looked at the fish and said,
“Maybe I should have eaten cat food instead.”

Then he dropped the fish and ran home.

From that day on, Milo stayed away from the aquarium.
